About Agastya Yogshala

Rooted in tradition, designed for modern wellness

Sanctuary of Peace & Healing

Agastya Yogshala is more than just a yoga center; it is a sacred space dedicated to uniting the mind, body, and soul. Through structured classes tailored for all experience levels, we help practitioners unlock physical mobility and emotional calmness.

We specialize in therapeutic yoga addressing conditions like sinus, BP, asthma, joint pain, PCOS, and migraine relief through ancient holistic methods.
